# Construction scope

Butson Towers contains existence constructions and exact scalar reductions.
The main campaign has **65 distinct completed real Hadamard orders: 34 new
to the best of the author's knowledge and 31 recovered known orders**.
The new periodic T127 ingredient is counted separately. The nine stated infinite
output families (eight Spence-source families and one cubic family) have
complete construction recipes for every nonnegative parameter;
their finite audited members carry individual known/new labels.

Exact algebraic Gram proofs establish every endpoint row inner product.
Full dense endpoint audits are recorded separately where executed. Source
verification, mathematical induction and proof-assistant verification remain
separately identified in their certificates.

The finite Butson-Hadamard and generalized-weighing classifications remain
in the original `hadamard` repository. Shared arithmetic support was copied
here to keep the construction project independent; those classifications
are outside this project's claims.
